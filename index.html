Avenir en commun publisher

A desktop publishing tool for the TikTok account @avenirencommun_2027, which posts one short, sourced explainer video per day about a measure of a French political programme.

How it works

  1. The account owner opens the tool on their computer and clicks Connect TikTok (TikTok Login Kit).
  2. They choose a finished video, review the caption, pick who can view it, set comment/Duet/Stitch options and keep the AI-generated content label enabled.
  3. After confirmation, the video is sent with the TikTok Content Posting API (Direct Post) and the tool shows the publishing status.

The tool is used only by the account owner, for that one account. It does not collect data about other TikTok users.
