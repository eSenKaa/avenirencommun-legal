Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Avenir en commun publisher ("the App"), a personal tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @avenirencommun_2027, handles data. Last updated: 8 November 2026.

1. Data we access

When the account owner authorizes the App with TikTok Login Kit, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) for that single account. It uses the scopes user.info.basic and video.publish.

2. How we use it

The token is used only to upload and publish the account owner's own videos to that same account, and to read the posting status. No data about other TikTok users is collected.

3. Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored locally on the account owner's computer. They are never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only as long as publishing is needed. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions), which makes the stored token unusable.

5. Contact

Questions: send a direct message to @avenirencommun_2027 on TikTok.
